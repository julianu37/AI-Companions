# Configuration

Where the configuration file is, how to edit it in game and what each option of the `[AI]` section does.

---

## Where the `.cfg` is

```
<Valheim>\BepInEx\config\AiCompanions.cfg
```

The file is **generated by BepInEx** the first time the mod loads, and it is written again when you change something
through the panels. It has about **2000 lines** and **~340 options** in total, split into several sections
(`[General]`, `[Gathering]`, `[Hunting]`, `[Speech]`, `[Advanced]`, `[AI]` and others).

> The file name is **no longer** the plugin GUID: until **1.8.2** it was
> `Maddhattr.OfflineCompanionsReborn.cfg` and **from 1.8.3 on** it is **`AiCompanions.cfg`**. The plugin **GUID**
> is still `Maddhattr.OfflineCompanionsReborn` — it is the mod's **identity** in the game and on the network (it is
> what ties the world, the save and the companions together), but it **no longer** names the file. The old `.cfg`
> **stays on the disk, ignored**: it can be deleted without fear.

> **Coming from 1.8.2 or older?** The settings **go back to the defaults** (that was the 1.8.3 change: the new
> file is created already with the default values) — including the **`ApiKey`**, which has to be pasted again.
> **Save, world, companions, houses and inventory are not affected** (the `HC_*` keys and the GUID did not change).
> The values of the old file are **not** migrated on purpose: the keys changed their names. **1.9.x keeps the file**
> and only moves the old fixed voice/language defaults to `auto`.

The **`[AI]`** section has **39 keys**. This is how it shows up in the file (in this example, with the **local
alternative** configured: `127.0.0.1`, `qwen2.5-3b-instruct` and the empty key — in the **default** the `BaseUrl` is
**Groq**, with the model `openai/gpt-oss-20b`, and the `ApiKey` is required):

```ini
[AI]
Enabled = true
BaseUrl = http://127.0.0.1:1234/v1
Model = qwen2.5-3b-instruct
ApiKey =
Temperature = 0.1
```

> The example above is the **local** alternative; in the **default** the first four lines are
> `Enabled = true`, `BaseUrl = https://api.groq.com/openai/v1`, `Model = openai/gpt-oss-20b` and
> `ApiKey = <your key>`. Until **1.8.2** the keys and the descriptions were in Portuguese (`Ligado`, `Modelo`…);
> **from 1.8.3 on** they are in **English** (`Enabled`, `Model`…), as in the table below.

## Editing in game

You do not need to open the `.cfg` in Notepad for the day to day:

| Panel | Key | What it edits |
|---|---|---|
| **Mod settings** | **`F8`** | **Every** option of the mod, in game |
| **AI panel** | **`I`** | The AI layer and **each companion**: the **AI tab** (summoner, LLM settings, global voice and push-to-talk) and **one tab per companion** (name, type, appearance, his voice and personality) |

### AI panel (key `I`)

The panel opens on the **AI tab** (no companion needs to be nearby) and has, at the top, **one tab per companion** —
when one of those tabs is open, the title becomes **his name**. The tabs scroll horizontally when there are many
companions. Everything is saved **right away**: the options that apply to everyone stay in the **AI tab**, and what
belongs to a companion stays in **his tab**.

#### AI tab

| What | How |
|---|---|
| **Summon a new companion** | Pick the **Tier** and press **Summon**: he is born **for free**, without going through the Trader, with the numbered type name ("Warrior 2"). The counter below shows how many are **on the scene**; the cap is `[General] MaxCompanions` (default **10**, counting **alive + dead + lost**) |
| **LLM settings** | The **On**, **Structured output** and **Show the reply in chat** toggles; the **Server URL**, **Model**, **API key** (**masked**) and **Prefix** fields; the **Temperature**, **Timeout (s)**, **Cooldown (s)** and **Target range (m)** sliders; and the **Apply** and **Test connection** buttons |
| **Pick your LLM provider** | The dropdown right **above the server URL**: choosing **Groq**, **LM Studio** or **OpenRouter** **fills in** the URL and the model (see the table below). **Custom (manual URL)** does not touch anything |
| **Volume** | The TTS slider (`[AI] TtsVolume`). The **speech speed** stays **only in the `F8` panel** (`[AI] TtsSpeed`) — the **Listen** button uses the value from there |
| **Push-to-talk** | The push-to-talk key (default **`V`**), the **Change** button to swap it and the **Voice orders** toggle |
| **Companion speaks / Speak AI replies / Speak mod lines / Free conversation** | The voice and conversation toggles (`TtsEnabled`, `TtsSpeakReplies`, `TtsSpeakModLines`, `Conversation`) |
| **Memory** | How many recent exchanges he remembers (0–12) |

> The text fields (URL, model, key, prefix) only apply after **Apply** — and on the **next message**, without
> restarting the game. The key never goes to the log.

> **Types:** the **dverger** (**Mage**, **Fire Mage**, **Ice Mage** and **Support**) are **combat** types: the ring
> shows only the combat/support actions and the **work** orders (by the **chat**, by the **voice** or by the
> **command key**) are refused with *"I fight, I don't do that work."*. To work, use the **Companion** type. It
> applies to the summoner's **Tier** and to the **Change tier** of the companion tab — see
> **[How it works](How-it-works#companion-types)**.

**Provider picker** (the caption **"Pick your LLM provider"**, with the same dropdown as the summoner's **Tier**) —
it **opens already on the provider your `.cfg` uses** and, when you pick one, it fills in the fields:

| Provider | URL | Model |
|---|---|---|
| **Groq** (default) | `https://api.groq.com/openai/v1` | `openai/gpt-oss-20b` |
| **LM Studio** | `http://127.0.0.1:1234/v1` | `qwen2.5-3b-instruct` |
| **OpenRouter** | `https://openrouter.ai/api/v1` | `openrouter/free` |
| **Custom (manual URL)** | *(touches nothing)* | *(touches nothing)* |

> Picking in the picker **only fills in the fields**: it applies after **Apply**, like any edit there. The
> **`ApiKey` is never touched** (neither filled in nor deleted) — it stays yours. And the picker **is not a new
> setting**: it **deduces** the provider from `[AI] BaseUrl` itself, so an **Ollama** URL or a server of yours shows
> up as **Custom (manual URL)** and nothing is overwritten — there is no second place that could diverge from the
> `.cfg`.

**"Create your free API key HERE" button** — on the **API key** row, in the right half: it **opens the browser** on
the key page of the chosen provider (**Groq** → `console.groq.com/keys`; **OpenRouter** → `openrouter.ai/keys`), so
you can grab the key without leaving the game. It **does not show up** on **LM Studio** or on **Custom** (those have
no key) and it **is not a picker**: clicking it does not change any state afterwards.

![The AI panel on the AI tab: the "Pick your LLM provider" dropdown on Groq, the companion summoner, the server URL, the model openai/gpt-oss-20b, the masked API key, the "Create your free API key HERE" button, Apply and Test connection](images/en-ai-panel.jpg)

#### Companion tab

| What | How |
|---|---|
| **Name** | The field with the current name + **Apply** (**Enter** also applies). The name counts in the HUD, in the hover, in the ring and in the orders by name. A **dead** companion cannot be renamed |
| **Tier** | The `<` `>` arrows pick it and **Change tier (respawns)** applies it, **with confirmation**: he is reborn as the new type and **everything stays** (name, inventory, equipment, appearance, voice, personality, home, bed, skills) — only the **job in progress** starts over |
| **Status** | **Alive** or **Dead** — and dying does **not** free a slot in the cap |
| **Call** | Brings the companion close to you: he walks over and, if he is too far away, comes back through the game's own recall |
| **Appearance** | Opens the **appearance editor** (right below) |
| **Personality** | The list with **"Follow the [AI] default"** first and the presets (**Loyal Viking**, **Sarcastic Mage**, **Sarcastic**, **Patient Guide**) — **picking already saves**. **In your own words** is the free field, applied by the **Apply** next to it |
| **Voice** | The list with **"Follow the [AI] default"** first and the **voices of the game's language** — picking already saves and applies to the **next line**; **Listen** plays a sample sentence right away. A voice stored for **another** language is not used (the default of the active language plays instead) |

> The voice and the personality chosen here belong to **this** companion, kept in **his ZDO**: they travel in the
> **tombstone** and come back on the respawn. Whoever is on "follow the default" obeys what is in the **AI tab** —
> changing the global one reaches everyone with no choice of their own. **Call** and **Appearance** are disabled
> with the companion dead.

![The AI panel on the tab of a companion: the tabs at the top (AI and one per companion), the Name and the Tier with Change tier (respawns), the Status, Call and Appearance, and Voice and personality of this companion with the voice and the Listen button](images/en-ai-panel-companions-editor.jpg)

**Appearance editor** (the **Appearance** button): gender, hair style, beard style, skin tone, hair tone and hair
shade, with a **3D figure** that changes live while you choose. **Apply** writes to the companion **without a
respawn**; **Cancel** closes without changing anything.

![The appearance editor: male/female gender, hair style, skin tone, hair tone and hair shade, with the 3D preview of the companion and the Cancel and Apply buttons](images/en-ai-panel-companions-editor-2.jpg)

The panel closes with **`I`**, with **`Esc`** or on the **X** — and `Esc` first leaves a text field and closes an
open list, then the panel. The **`I`** key is ignored while you type (chat, console, trader search, the panel's own
fields) and with the **inventory**, the **map**, the **menu**, the **`F8`** panel or the starter companion's picker
open; and the panel **closes itself** when you die or leave the world.

> What is not in the `I` panel stays in **`F8`**, which edits **every** section of the `.cfg` with the same key
> names — the hint in the panel footer says exactly that.

## `[AI]` section — every key

There are **39 keys**, all in **English** (the mod's **native language** since 1.8.3 — the `.cfg` and the
descriptions included). The default values are the ones from the source code: if you never touched anything, this is
what is in force.

> **`[AI]` is the `.cfg` section; `[IA]` is the log tag.** The mod's **log lines** still start with `[IA]`
> (`[IA] Prompt:`, `[IA] Tempo:`, `[IA] Request #…`) — it is the message prefix in `LogOutput.log`, not a
> configuration path. Do not look for `[IA]` in the `.cfg`: there the section is `[AI]`.

| Key | Default | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| `Enabled` | `true` | Turns the text orders on. Off = nothing is sent to the model |
| `BaseUrl` | `https://api.groq.com/openai/v1` | LLM endpoint. `/chat/completions` is added on its own. The default is **Groq** (which asks for the `ApiKey`); the alternatives are **LM Studio** (`http://127.0.0.1:1234/v1`) or **Ollama** (`http://127.0.0.1:11434/v1`) to run everything locally, and **OpenRouter** (`https://openrouter.ai/api/v1`), which works but has a slower free tier. See **[LLM (LM Studio, Groq or OpenRouter)](LLM)** |
| `Model` | `openai/gpt-oss-20b` | Model name, as the server lists it. `openai/gpt-oss-20b` is the default (Groq, free tier). Alternatives: `openrouter/free` on OpenRouter; on LM Studio use the name it shows (e.g. `qwen2.5-3b-instruct`) |
| `ApiKey` | *(empty)* | API key of the server. **Required on Groq** (the free one comes from `console.groq.com/keys`) and also on OpenRouter (`openrouter.ai/keys`); empty for local LM Studio/Ollama |
| `Temperature` | `0.1` | How much the model improvises. Keep it low so the same order always gives the same JSON |
| `TimeoutSeconds` | `30` | Seconds to wait for the model. The request runs in the background |
| `Prefix` | *(empty)* | Empty = every chat message goes to the model. E.g. `/c` requires the prefix |
| `ShowReplyInChat` | `true` | Shows the model's raw reply in the chat (useful in tests) |
| `CooldownSeconds` | `3` | Minimum time between two orders |
| `TargetRange` | `40` | How far (m) to look for the creature named in an attack order. The one you aim at always counts |
| `Personality` | *(empty)* | The personality **in your own words** (up to 600 characters). It wins over the preset |
| `PersonalityPreset` | *(empty)* | The `.cfg` value is an id: `viking`, `mago`, `sarcastico`, `guia` or `custom`. Empty/unknown = the default (loyal and practical). The panel shows the name in the game's language |
| `Memory` | `4` | How many recent exchanges he remembers and sends along with the next message. `0` = no memory (ceiling 12) |
| `Conversation` | `true` | Answer free conversation when the message is not an order |
| `StructuredOutput` | `true` | Asks the server for the JSON format (`response_format`/`json_schema`). It is what allows small models |
| `SttTest` | `false` | Test: runs the Whisper recognition alone ~25 s after loading and writes it to the log |
| `SttModel` | `whisper\ggml-small-q5_1.bin` | Whisper model (relative to the plugin folder or an absolute path) |
| `SttWavTest` | `whisper\jfk.wav` | WAV that `modia_stt_teste` transcribes. **The package does not bring that file** — point it at a WAV of yours |
| `VoiceEnabled` | `true` | Turns the push-to-talk on |
| `VoiceKey` | `V` | Push-to-talk key |
| `VoiceLanguage` | `auto` | Language of the speech (Whisper code). `auto` follows the **game's language** (PT-BR, EN, ES, FR, IT); an explicit code always wins. Empty also means `auto` |
| `VoiceMaxSeconds` | `10` | Recording ceiling, in seconds |
| `VoiceSilenceThreshold` | `0.01` | How loud the audio has to be to count as speech |
| `VoiceInitialPrompt` | *(empty)* | Whisper initial prompt: biases the recognition towards the order words. Empty = the **orders vocabulary of the game's language**, written by the mod; anything you type here wins (`none` turns the bias off) |
| `VoiceDevice` | `0` | Microphone index (the log prints the list) |
| `VoiceShowText` | `true` | Shows in the chat/HUD what the microphone heard |
| `PanelKey` | `I` | Key that opens the **AI panel** |
| `TtsEnabled` | `true` | He **speaks** the replies (local TTS, Kokoro) |
| `TtsVoice` | `0` | Which voice: `0` = **automatic** (the default voice of the game's language); an explicit id **of that language** wins (`42` pf_dora, `43` pm_alex, `44` pm_santa in Portuguese; `3` af_heart, `2` af_bella, `6` af_nicole, `14` am_fenrir, `16` am_michael, `18` am_puck in English; `28` ef_dora, `29` em_alex in Spanish; `30` ff_siwis in French; `35` if_sara, `36` im_nicola in Italian) |
| `TtsSpeed` | `1` | Speech speed |
| `TtsThreads` | `4` | CPU cores for the synthesis |
| `TtsVolume` | `1` | Voice volume (0 = muted) |
| `TtsModel` | `tts\kokoro\model.int8.onnx` | Kokoro model. Voices/tokens/espeak in the **same folder** |
| `TtsLanguage` | `auto` | Language of the spoken text (Kokoro code). `auto` follows the **game's language** (`pt-br`, `en-us`, `es`, `fr`, `it`); an explicit code always wins. **Never empty** — the model requires a language |
| `TtsCache` | `true` | Keeps the lines already synthesized |
| `TtsSpeakReplies` | `true` | Speaks the model's reply |
| `TtsSpeakModLines` | `true` | Speaks the mod's line that appears in the chat (it wins in an order) |
| `TtsReverbZoneMix` | `0` | How much of the world's reverb zones goes into the voice. `0` = dry; `0.19` = a subtle "space" |
| `EcoDiag` | `false` | Audio diagnostics: writes to the log (once per session) the `AudioListener`, the reverb zones and how the game's sources are configured |

## Language

**Five languages: Portuguese (Brazil), English, Spanish, French and Italian** — and **English is the mod's native
language**:

- **The game's language decides everything**: the interface (914 keys), the companion's speech (221 lines), the
  personality presets, the LLM contract and the **voice and the speech recognition**. With the game in
  **Portuguese**, the names of the **`F8`** options and the tabs appear in **PT** (`Ligado`, `Modelo`, `VozTecla`…);
  with the game in **English**, the native names appear (`Enabled`, `Model`, `VoiceKey`…); Spanish, French and
  Italian have their own file. The **`I` panel** is translated the same way. **Changing the language does not need a
  restart**: the panels rebuild on the next open and the HUD, the speech and the TTS/STT follow along.
- **`[AI] VoiceLanguage`, `TtsLanguage` and `TtsVoice` are automatic by default** (`auto`, `auto`, `0`): the mod
  resolves them from the game's language. Voices per language (the default first): `pt-br` → `pf_dora`, `pm_alex`,
  `pm_santa`; `en-us` → `af_heart`, `af_bella`, `af_nicole`, `am_fenrir`, `am_michael`, `am_puck`; `es` → `ef_dora`,
  `em_alex`; `fr` → `ff_siwis`; `it` → `if_sara`, `im_nicola`.
- **An explicit value always wins** — and the `F8` and the `I` panel show the **effective** value, e.g.
  `Automatic (en-US · af_heart)`.
- **In the `.cfg`**, the **keys and the descriptions are in English, always** — it **does not** change with the
  game's language (the same goes for `package/CONFIGURATION.md`). That is why the **key name is the English one**,
  even with the game in Portuguese: the key is the identifier of the option, not the label you read on the screen.
- **New languages come in through a file**, without touching the code: `Translations/<Language>.json` (914 keys)
  brings the interface texts (including the names of the `F8` options and tabs) and `Speech/<Language>.json` (221
  lines) brings the **companion's lines** — the same scheme. **English does not need a file**, because the keys
  **are** the text.
- **Languages the Kokoro model does not speak** (German, Russian, Polish, Turkish, Korean, Swedish and Ukrainian)
  use the **English voice** — the interface and the speech can still be translated; the audio is English.

## Weight and carry capacity

The companion's weight limit is **from the mod**, not from the game: vanilla does **not** impose a ceiling on a
`Humanoid` that is not a player. Two keys decide the behaviour and **both are the base value** — items with a
**carry bonus** add **on top** of them:

| Section / key | Default | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| `[General] MaxCarryWeight` | `300` | The **base** weight ceiling of the companion (50–2000). It is the `x/300` of the panel and of the **HUD** bar and what triggers **`Encumbered`** (slow walking). The **Megingjord** (+150) adds on top: with the default, his effective ceiling becomes **450** |
| `[Gathering] OverweightThreshold` | `298` | The **base** weight at which the **gathering** companion (wood, stone, ore, forage and the garden plot) stops picking things up and heads home to unload (50–500). With the **Megingjord** in the bag he only heads back around **448**. **Hunt and fish do not use this key** (they have their own bag-full options) |

> **The bonus counts with the item anywhere in the bag** — no need to equip it in the utility slot (on a `Humanoid`
> that is not a player it does not even survive the reload). And it counts **only once**: two equal belts **do not
> stack**, as the game treats equipment effects. The value is read from the **asset**
> (`SE_Stats.m_addMaxCarryWeight`), never from a fixed `150` — a belt modded to +250 works the same.
>
> The effective ceiling counts in **`Encumbered`**, in the `x/450` of the **companion panel** and of the **HUD** bar,
> in the refusal of **loot** and of the **pickup from the ground**, in the **smith**, in the **upkeep**, in the
> "bag full" of **fishing/hunting** and in the "I'm full" warning. Each change of bonus writes **one line** to the
> log (never per frame): `[Carry] "Thorin": +150 de capacidade (Megingjord) - teto 450`.

## What **we** changed compared to the upstream

Besides the translation and the AI layer, the project adjusted a few upstream defaults. If you came from Offline
Companions Reborn, this is what changed in value:

| Section / key | Upstream default | Our default | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| `[Advanced] GatheringSearchRadius` | 30 | **50** | Following you, the companion can be 25 m away and the target 25 m on the other side — the search ring has to cover ~50 m |
| `[Gathering] FollowRange` | 10 | **25** | He works up to 25 m from you, instead of sticking to you |
| `[Gathering] ForageFollowRange` | 10 | **20** | Foraging is still "what you walked past", but with room |
| `[Gathering] Range` | 40 | **60** | Gathering reach with **Follow off** |
| `[Advanced] GatheringDropRadius` | 8 | **12** | A tree can fall further than 8 m; the drops were being left behind |
| `[Looting] CollectRadius` | *(new)* | **10** | Radius of the "pick up the items on the ground" order |
| `[General] MaxCompanions` | *(new)* | **10** | Companion cap per player, counting **alive + dead + lost** (dying does not free a slot) |
| `[Speech] Chatter` | a choice: `Off`/`Rare`/`Normal`/`Often` (default `Normal`) | **`true`/`false`** (default `false`) | Upstream turns the random chatter into four levels - a line every 1.5–3 min on `Normal`. Here it is a plain **on/off**: off (the default) there is no chatter from the timer at all, on it comes back every `[Speech] MinInterval`/`MaxInterval` (15–30 s). The useful lines (orders, problems, the job he is doing) are said either way |
| `[Death] RespawnDelay` | 300 | **30** | Seconds before a dead companion comes back on its own (with `AutoRespawn` on), and 30 is the shortest value the setting accepts. Waiting five minutes only kept it away for longer: the tombstone is still how you get it back - with its gear |
| `[Advanced] UiDiag` | *(new)* | **`false`** | UI diagnostics (clicks/layers): opt-in, no cost when off |
| `[AI] EcoDiag` | *(new)* | **`false`** | Companion audio diagnostics: opt-in |

> The five gathering radii are migrated automatically **only** if they are exactly on the old value. A value you
> chose by hand is **never** overwritten.

The two diagnostics (`UiDiag` and `EcoDiag`) are **opt-in**: off they write nothing and cost nothing; on they write
**once per session** and they are the first thing to look at when a click or a sound looks odd.

## Seeing everything in the `F8` panel

The **`F8`** panel shows **every** section of the `.cfg`, with the same value ranges. The **names** of the options
and of the **tabs** appear in the **game's language**: in Portuguese you read `Ligado`, `Modelo`, `VozTecla`…; in
English, the native names (`Enabled`, `Model`, `VoiceKey`…) — it is the key itself, because English needs no
translation. It is the quickest way to check what is in force right now, without leaving the game.

![The F8 panel open on the [AI] section, with the options of the AI layer](images/en-f8-panel-advanced-configurations.jpg)

> The capture is from a build with the **English keys** (`Conversation`, `StructuredOutput`, `VoiceEnabled`,
> `TtsVoice`…). Since **1.8.3** the `.cfg` is in English; with the game in **Portuguese** the panel keeps showing
> the names in PT.

> The mod's panels sit **above the game HUD** in the drawing order — it is what guarantees that a click on the panel
> reaches the panel, and not the HUD.
